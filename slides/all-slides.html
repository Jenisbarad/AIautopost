Gemma 4 QAT Models

Optimizing Model Compression
@dailyainewsone1/3
WHAT HAPPENED
Google has introduced Gemma 4 QAT models, which optimize model compression for mobile and laptop efficiency.

The models use quantization-aware training (QAT) to reduce the size of AI models.

This allows for faster and more efficient deployment of AI models on devices.

The Gemma 4 QAT models were announced on 05 Jun 2026.
