SMEs may miss deadlines
@dailyainewsone1/3
WHAT HAPPENED
Event date: 06 May 2026

The EU AI Act has identified evidence gaps that deployers must address before 2 August 2026.

Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) may struggle to meet these deadlines.

The Act requires deployers to provide evidence of their AI systems' compliance with EU regulations.

The evidence gaps include the lack of transparency and explainability in AI decision-making.
